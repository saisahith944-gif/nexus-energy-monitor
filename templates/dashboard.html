HOW LIMITS WORK

Compare fairly. Act early.

The expected limit is calculated from each room's historical monthly average and the configured increase percentage. Warning and critical thresholds are then applied to that limit.

Normal below the warning threshold
Warning at or above warning threshold
Critical above the expected limit, or at the configured critical threshold
No recent reading no saved sample within 45 days

High use is a signal to inspect, not proof of wastage. No smart meters are connected in this demo.

Carbon estimate = recorded kWh × configured grid emissions factor; adjust factor for your region.
